# Blue & I

**Find your people at Whitman.** Blue & I is a friend-finding and activity-partner app made only for Whitman College students. Use it to find someone to study with, hit the gym, go hiking, or check out a club.

> **Status: idea stage.** This repo introduces the concept. Code and new ideas will be added as the project grows.

## Why

College can be surprisingly isolating, even on a small campus. Blue & I has one goal: **get people off their screens and meeting in person.** It is not a dating app. It's for finding the friend who also wants to go climbing on Saturday.

The name comes from the Whitman **Blues** and is meant to sound like "you and I."

## Planned features

- **Whitman-only sign-up.** Requires a verified `@whitman.edu` email.
- **Interest & activity profiles.** Study habits, sports, outdoors, music, clubs, and availability.
- **Matching by activity, not looks.** Find people who want to do the same thing at the same time.
- **Activity posts.** "Anyone want to hike Saturday?" posts that others can join.
- **Messaging** to make plans.

## Planned tech

React frontend, Flask (Python) API, SQL database, and `@whitman.edu` email verification.
